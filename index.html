GET TO KNOW ME

About Me

My journey into cybersecurity is just beginning, but my passion is limitless.

I am Afolayan Dorcas Oluwabukola, a 100 Level Cybersecurity student committed to learning, growing, and discovering the many possibilities within cybersecurity.

I believe cybersecurity is not only about protecting systems and information. It is also about protecting people, educating communities, and creating a safer digital future.

One of the things I am especially passionate about is encouraging girls and young women to explore technology and confidently pursue careers in cybersecurity.

Through continuous learning, certifications, projects, and content creation, I hope to document my journey while inspiring others who may also be wondering where to begin.

This is only the beginning. More is coming.
